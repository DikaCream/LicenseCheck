This week we share a poem that we love:

The harbor lights are folding into rain,
the cranes stand still like herons made of iron,
and every ferry carries home a name
the tide forgot to write upon the shoreline.

I count the windows burning on the hill,
one for each sailor who did not come back,
and the lighthouse keeps its promise still:
a white word spoken to the black.
