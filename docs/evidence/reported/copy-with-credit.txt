Tonight we read "Harbor Lights" by Mira Okafor, shared under CC BY-NC 4.0. Original: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DikaCream/LicenseCheck/main/docs/evidence/original/harbor-lights.txt

The harbor lights are folding into rain,
the cranes stand still like herons made of iron,
and every ferry carries home a name
the tide forgot to write upon the shoreline.
