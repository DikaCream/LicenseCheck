Coastal Moodboard, entry 14 (fictional test fixture for LicenseCheck)

The harbor lights are folding into rain,
the cranes stand still like herons made of iron,
and every ferry carries home a name
the tide forgot to write upon the shoreline.

Poem "Harbor Lights" by Mira Okafor (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DikaCream/LicenseCheck/main/docs/evidence/original/harbor-lights.txt).
